## Why Tadpole OS?

**AI-Tadpole-OS** is built for operators who demand total sovereignty, performance, and transparency over their AI infrastructure. Unlike cloud-locked orchestrators, Tadpole OS runs locally, persists institutional memory across sessions, and executes complex multi-agent workflows under strict budget, security, and governance barriers.

### The Dual-Plane Sovereign Hierarchy

The engine operates under the **Dual-Plane Sovereign Hierarchy** ([Governance Guide](./GOVERNANCE_ROLE_GUIDE.md)):
1. **Plane 1 — Sovereign Codebase Lifecycle Engine**: Recursively knows, operates, diagnoses, fixes, maintains, and improves the `AI-Tadpole-OS` codebase over time using `ADG v2`, `parity_guard.py`, and `Agent 99`.
2. **Plane 2 — Company-Agnostic SMB AI Digital Twin**: Executes synthetic business workflows, customer enquiry SOPs, and practice runs within isolated company workspaces (`Mock-1-Product`, `Mock-2-Service`) protected by the `FR-11` permission firewall (see the [SMB Live Operations Guide](./SMB_LIVE_OPERATION_GUIDE.md) and [Mock SMB Digital Twin Spec](./specs/mock-smb-digital-twin.md)).
