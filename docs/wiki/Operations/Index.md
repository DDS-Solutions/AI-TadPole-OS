## Key Concepts

- **[[Glossary#department-lead|Department Lead]] [Tier 1: Operator]**: The orchestrating agent that breaks down your goals and recruits specialists.
- **[[Glossary#project-workspace|Project Workspace]] [Tier 1: Operator]**: A collaborative local directory grouping virtual employees toward a single goal.
- **[[Glossary#private-swarm-environment|Private Swarm Environment]] [Tier 1: Operator]**: The secure, local-first runtime where all processing happens on your hardware.
- **[[Glossary#department-scope|Department Scope]] [Tier 1: Operator]**: Audience targeting that restricts chat broadcasts to a specific workspace or folder.
